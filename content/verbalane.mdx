## Goal

Verbalane helps language learners practice with short dialogue-based stories built from real news topics. Instead of
presenting news as a monologue, each story becomes a conversation with line-by-line audio, contextual vocabulary hints,
and progress tracking.

<Tags />

![Two women speaking French over coffee on a city street](/verbalane/title.png)

## Approach

The project focuses on making authentic language input approachable. Stories are short, structured as realistic
dialogues, and designed for learners who want to understand both the language and the context behind current events.

Audio is available for every line, so learners can move naturally between reading and listening. Vocabulary hints keep
support close to the sentence where it is needed instead of interrupting the reading flow.
